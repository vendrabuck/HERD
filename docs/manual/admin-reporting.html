Utilization reporting

When leadership asks "is the lab actually being used, and by whom?", this is the page that answers it — reservation-hours, broken out by user, device, team, type, and template, with CSV for the spreadsheet crowd.

One job

Answer "who used what, for how long" over a date window. It's admin only — if you don't see Reporting in the nav, you're not an admin.

Finding it

Where it lives

Click Reporting in the top nav. It defaults to a 30-day window.

  1. Window picker. 7 days, 30 days, or Custom (two date pickers, UTC-anchored so the numbers match the backend regardless of your timezone).
  2. Headline cards. Total reservation-hours, reservation count, and execution runs for the window.
  3. Daily trend. Reservation-hours per day. A booking spanning midnight is counted on both days correctly.
  4. Download CSV. Per-table export for the By User, By Device, By Template, and Fleet Utilization tables.
  5. Fleet Utilization. Every inventory device with a utilization rate against the full window, current status, and an "Idle only" filter for zero-booking devices. Unlike the tables below, this section counts active as well as completed reservations by default, so in-flight usage shows up in a window that includes today.

Two things to know first

Reading the numbers honestly

  • Reservations are clamped to the window. A 30-day booking seen through a 7-day window contributes 7 days, not 30. The numbers are about this period, not lifetime totals.
  • Cancelled and failed bookings are excluded by default — only real usage counts.
  • "Execution runs" can read n/a if the execution service is briefly unreachable. The rest of the report still renders; that one card just can't be sourced.

Scoping it

Choosing a window

Pick 7 days for a weekly standup number, 30 days (the default) for a monthly review, or Custom for a quarter, a fiscal period, or a specific incident window. The date pickers are UTC-anchored, so what you select is exactly what the backend filters on — no off-by-a-day timezone surprises in the export.

The breakdowns

The fleet card and the five tables

01

By User

Owner name, hours, reservation count. "Who is the lab's heaviest user?"

02

By Device

Device (with template), hours, count. Spot the over- and under-used gear.

03

By Group (cost center)

User-group hours. People in several groups count against each; ungrouped land in Ungrouped.

04

By Topology Type

PHYSICAL vs CLOUD split of hours and count.

05

By Template

Template name, hours, count. Devices deleted since their booking show up under template Unknown — expected, not a bug.

06

Fleet Utilization

Per-device utilization rate over the whole window, idle-device view, and fleet-wide summary. The denominator is always the full window; a box down for maintenance shows a low rate with its current status next to it.

For the spreadsheet

Exporting CSV

Each of By User, By Device, and By Template has a Download CSV button. User and device exports come straight from the server; the template export is built in your browser from the same data the table shows.

Hitting the CSV endpoint directly with section=template returns a 422 on purpose — that rollup depends on inventory and is computed client-side. Use the button.
